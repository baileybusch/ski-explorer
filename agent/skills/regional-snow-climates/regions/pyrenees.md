# Pyrenees (Spain, Andorra, France)

Tags: **[src]** = linked source; **[GK]** = general knowledge, approximate. Optional region — thinner sourcing.

## Climate type
- Mixed: **Atlantic-influenced west/north side** (French side, Val d'Aran) vs. **Mediterranean-influenced east/south** (Catalonia, Andorra) [GK].

## Snow-to-liquid and density
- Atlantic storms: mild and often wet/dense; Mediterranean easterly storms ("retorno"): can be heavy and dense [GK, approx.]. Cold northerly flows bring lighter snow to the north-facing French side and Val d'Aran [GK].

## Storm setups and flow
- **NW/N Atlantic flow**: favors the French side and Val d'Aran (Baqueira, which sits on the north-facing Atlantic slope) [GK].
- **Easterly/southeasterly Mediterranean flow** with a low over the western Mediterranean: favors Andorra and eastern Catalan resorts [GK].
- Genoa lows can be triggered by Atlantic air entering the Mediterranean through the Carcassonne gap between the Pyrenees and the Massif Central [src: Wikipedia, Genoa low].

## Snow-killers
- Southerly foehn on the French side; warm SW flow with rain [GK].
- Long dry anticyclonic spells [GK].

## Elevation and freezing levels
- Bases ~1,500–2,100 m, summits ~2,500–2,900 m [GK, approx.].

## Seasonality
- Main season Dec–Apr; snowfall highly variable year to year [GK].

## Powder-day profile
- Cold NW storms for the French side/Aran; Mediterranean storms for Andorra. Strong sun quickly affects south aspects [GK].

## ENSO and teleconnections
- NAO matters more than ENSO; negative NAO often brings more storms to Iberia [GK].

## Avalanche and snowpack
- Mixed; sun crusts and wind slabs common [GK].

## Example resorts
- **Baqueira Beret** (Spain, Val d'Aran), **Grandvalira** (Andorra), **Cauterets** (France) [GK].
